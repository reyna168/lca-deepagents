# Weeknight Pasta

## Ingredients
- 12 oz pasta (spaghetti, penne, or your favorite shape)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and red pepper flakes; cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Add cooked pasta to the sauce along with 1/2 cup reserved pasta water. Toss to combine, adding more pasta water if needed.
5. Remove from heat, stir in fresh basil, and serve topped with Parmesan.

**Prep time:** 10 min | **Cook time:** 15 min | **Serves:** 4
